🚀 QUICK INSTALLATION (5 MINUTES)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. LOG INTO SHOPIFY
   → Go to your Shopify Admin panel
   → Navigate to: Online Store > Themes

2. UPLOAD THEME
   → Click "Upload theme" button
   → Select: pellair-oils-theme.zip
   → Wait for upload to complete

3. PUBLISH THEME
   → Once uploaded, click "Publish" on the new theme
   → Your store is now live with the Pellair Oils theme!

4. CONFIGURE METAFIELDS (REQUIRED)
   → Go to: Settings > Custom Data > Products
   → Add these metafields for brand/accord filtering:
     
     • Brand (custom.brand) - Single line text
     • Accord (custom.accord) - Single line text
     • Concentration (custom.concentration) - Single line text
     • Top Notes (custom.top_notes) - Multi-line text
     • Middle Notes (custom.middle_notes) - Multi-line text
     • Base Notes (custom.base_notes) - Multi-line text
     • Minimum Order (custom.minimum_order) - Integer

5. ADD YOUR FIRST PRODUCT
   → Products > Add product
   → Fill in title, description, price, images
   → In Metafields section, add:
     - Brand: "Guerlain" (or your brand)
     - Accord: "floral" (or woody, oriental, etc.)
     - Concentration: "EDP"
     - Notes: Top, Middle, Base notes
   → Save product

   → Decide to approve or decline

   → Remove tag: "pending_approval"
   → Optionally send welcome email

4. CUSTOMER ACTIVATED
   → Can now checkout
   → Sees B2B pricing (if configured)
   → Full access to bulk ordering
